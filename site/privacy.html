Summary

Planetola Radio FM does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Everything you set up in the app stays on your device.

Data stored on your device

The app keeps the following information only in its local storage on your phone:

  • the name you optionally enter in the profile;
  • the interface language and the chosen start page;
  • your favorite stations and liked tracks (track title, artist, station, city and the time you saved them).

This data never leaves the device. It is deleted together with the app when you uninstall it.

Network connections

To play radio, the app connects to third-party servers. These connections are the same as opening a website: the server sees the IP address of your device and basic connection details, but the app does not send any identifier, name or other information about you.

  • Radio stations. The app streams audio directly from the station's own server and, while a station is playing, periodically reads the current track title that the station embeds in its stream (ICY metadata). The app does not download or record audio.
  • Radio Browser (api.radio-browser.info), the open community directory the station list is built from. When you start a station, the app notifies the directory that the station was played so its popularity counter is updated. This request contains the station's identifier only, nothing about you.

The station list itself is bundled with the app and is not downloaded while you use it.

Permissions

  • Microphone (Android only). The mascot dances to the music. On Android the system component that measures the loudness of the audio being played requires the microphone permission. The app never records audio and never transmits it; you can deny the permission and the mascot will dance to a fixed beat instead. On iOS no microphone permission is requested.
  • Background audio. Used only to keep the radio playing when the screen is locked or another app is in front.

Children

The app does not collect data from anyone, including children. Radio stations broadcast their own programming, which the app does not control or filter.
