Contact

Write to support. Please mention your device model, iOS version and the app version from the Profile tab. We reply within a few days.

Frequently asked questions

A station does not play. Stations run their own servers and sometimes go offline or move. Try another station from the same city; the list is refreshed with app updates.

No track title is shown. Only stations that embed the title in their stream can show it. The player says so when a station sends nothing.

The radio stops when the screen locks. Make sure the app is allowed to play in the background and that Low Power Mode does not pause it. If it still stops, tell us the station and the time, it helps a lot.

How do I add my city or station? Send the station name and the stream URL; stations listed in Radio Browser can be added quickly.

Where are my favorites stored? Only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Station data

The station list comes from the open community directory Radio Browser. The app streams public broadcasts published by the stations themselves and does not download or record audio.
