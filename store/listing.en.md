## Description (≤4000)
Planetola Radio FM turns the whole planet into one radio dial. Spin a 3D globe, bring a city to the center and start listening to its live stations in a tap.

LISTEN AROUND THE WORLD
• 6,600+ internet radio stations from 338 cities and capitals around the world
• Full station lists for every European country, Russia and the USA: 18,000+ more stations
• A real 3D Earth with NASA imagery: rotate it with a finger, pinch to zoom, tap a city
• Prefer lists? Browse cities by region, countries, or search by name, tag, language, codec and bitrate

KNOW WHAT'S PLAYING
• The player shows the current artist and track title when a station broadcasts it
• Liked a track? Tap the heart and it's saved with the station and the time
• Copy the track title with one tap to look it up anywhere

MAKE IT YOURS
• Star stations to build your own list of favorites
• Choose what opens at start: the globe, the city list, a specific city or your favorite stations
• Interface in 28 languages

RADIO THAT KEEPS PLAYING
• Background playback with the screen locked or while you use other apps; resumes after a phone call
• Station and current track on the lock screen and in CarPlay; next and previous buttons switch stations
• Station details at a glance: codec, bitrate, broadcast language and tags
• A dancing beaver with a boombox keeps you company

PRIVACY
No accounts, no ads, no tracking. Your favorites and settings stay on your device.

Station data comes from the open community directory Radio Browser. Stations broadcast their own programs and streams; availability may change.

## App Review Notes
The app plays publicly available internet radio streams that the stations themselves publish in the open Radio Browser directory (https://www.radio-browser.info). Nothing is downloaded, recorded or redistributed; audio is streamed directly from each station's server. The "audio" background mode is used only to continue playback when the app is in the background. No sign-in is required to test any feature.
